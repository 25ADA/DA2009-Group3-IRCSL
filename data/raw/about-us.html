About Us.

The IRCSL safeguards policy holders through supervisory control of Insurance Companies. Professionals with experience at IRCSL advise the government on policy and laws that would make the industry remain viable, able to meet its commitments to policy holders by helping them overcome risks.

                                                        Assistant Director (Supervision)

                                                        • Register as insurers persons carrying on insurance business in Sri Lanka.
                                                        • Register persons as insurance brokers.
                                                        • Advise the Government on the development and regulation of the insurance industry.
                                                        • Implement the policies and programmes of the Government with respect to the insurance industry.
                                                        • Employ such officers and servants as may be necessary for the purpose of exercising, performing and discharging the powers, duties and functions of the Board.
                                                        • Acquire and hold any property, movable or immovable and sell, lease, mortgage or otherwise dispose of the same.
                                                        • Enter into all such contracts as may be necessary for the exercise, performance and discharge of its powers, duties and functions.
                                                        • Carry out such other acts as may be necessary for the due exercise, discharge and performance of its powers, duties and functions under this act.
                                                        IRCSL Organization chart

                                                        Key Functions 

                                                        • Inquire into complaints referred by aggrieved claimants relating to unsatisfactory settlement of claims or complaints regarding misconduct/misappropriation by an intermediary.
                                                        • Inquire into matters referred either internally or by way of complaints relating to any alleged irregularity/noncompliance in terms of the RII Act No. 43 of 2000 as amended and/or Rules and Regulations made there under.

                                                        Key Functions 

                                                        • Provides legal advisory services to the Commission on regulatory and compliance matters.
                                                        • Drafts and formulates Regulations, Rules, Directions, Determinations, Circulars, Orders, Guidelines, and other subordinate legislation required under the Regulation of Insurance Industry Act, No. 43 of 2000 (RII Act), and recommends amendments to the Act and related subordinate legislation to strengthen and enhance the insurance regulatory framework.
                                                        • Initiates and oversees enforcement actions under the RII Act against regulated entities for non-compliance with the Act and applicable subordinate legislation.
                                                        • Evaluates and processes applications for the registration of insurers and other regulated persons in accordance with the provisions of the RII Act.
                                                        • Performs the functions of the Information Officer appointed under the Right to Information Act, No. 12 of 2016.
                                                        • Evaluates applications for the appointment of Directors and Principal Officers of insurers.
                                                        • Reviews agreements, contracts, and Memoranda of Understanding (MOUs) referred to the Legal Division.
                                                        • Initiates and defends legal proceedings on behalf of the Commission and , safeguarding interest of the Commission.

                                                        Key Functions 

                                                        Supervision Division monitors the solvency of insurance companies and financial stability of insurance companies and broking companies by periodical review of their financial reports. In addition, it carries out on-site inspections of insurance companies and brokering companies. Further, steps are currently being taken to introduce “Risk Based Supervision” for the supervisory mechanism replacing the existing “Rules Based” supervisory system.

                                                        Key Functions 

                                                        • To have a robust administrative system, improved internal systems and controls, ensure competent staff are recruited and retained while enhancing their knowledge and skills to enable them to perform their duties effectively and efficiently. Formulate administrative and related guidelines, procure value-adding goods, services and consultancy services and provide a healthy and safe working environment whilst boosting the morale of staff to perform their duties proactively and efficiently.

                                                        Key Functions 

                                                        • Enhance the financial literacy levels of the consumers on Insurance through Strategic Communication.
                                                        • Conducts awareness programmes for the general public in order to impart knowledge and increase awareness on insurance related matters, with a view of facilitating the development of the insurance industry.
                                                        • Analysis of progressive developments in other jurisdictions, new trends in the global insurance industry with a view of implementing global best practices in the local insurance industry.
                                                        • Create positive and productive relationships with the local/international stakeholders with a view to facilitate developing the local insurance industry.
                                                        • Analysis of various factors, which drive the performance of insurance industry through market research and make suggestions to develop the insurance industry.

                                                        Key Functions 

                                                        • Provide Actuarial advice and support to the Commission and the management.
                                                        • Ensure a robust regulatory & supervisory framework for the insurance industry from an actuarial perspective.
                                                        • Review Statutory Returns (Quarterly and Annual) filed by the companies and monitor the compliance with rules and communicate the shortcomings and make clarifications.
                                                        • Review Actuarial certificates of long-term insurance products, Reinsurance arrangements, Regulatory reporting formats and Applications filed by new entrants to the market.
                                                        • Implement and develop templates/models for Own Risk and Solvency Assessments and Enterprise Risk Management.
                                                        • Review current Rules, Regulations and Guidelines on insurance products and processes.
                                                        • Provide actuarial support on on-site inspections conducted by the IRCSL.
                                                        • Provide observations on the compliance regarding claims as and when required.

                                                        Key Functions 

                                                        • Conduct assessment of risk mitigatory measures on AML/CFT/CPF of institutions coming under the purview of IRCSL, including on-site supervision/off-site monitoring
                                                        • Develop/maintain AML/CFT/CPF supervisory manuals and develop supervisory plans based on risk assessment
                                                        • Ensure compliance with relevant provisions of the national policy on AML/CFT/CPF
                                                        • Conduct awareness sessions and activities to enhance the sector on AML/CFT/CPF requirements/trends and developments and carry out all matters relating to IRCSL’s responsibilities under its scope
                                                        • Liaising with FIU of the Central Bank, Financial Action Task force, Ministry of Justice, Foreign counterparts and other related parties as an when necessary
                                                        • Capacity building, guiding, training of staff on AML/CFT/CPF and other regulatory areas assigned
                                                        • Carry out review of insurance products and facilitate product clearance process

                                                        Key Functions 

                                                        • Strengthen regulatory and supervisory framework for market conduct supervision to ensure fair treatment of policyholders.
                                                        • Monitor and identify unlawful or inappropriate practices in pre-sale and post-sale processes through offsite surveillance and risk-based onsite examinations and recommend appropriate corrective actions.
                                                        • Conduct offsite risk assessments, onsite examinations, and thematic reviews of insurers to evaluate their compliance with market conduct guidelines and industry best practices.
                                                        • Enhance clarity and consistency of insurance policies by standardizing key wordings based on market conduct assessments.
                                                        • Promote ethical business conduct through knowledge sharing initiatives and support insurers to strengthen the implementation of market conduct guidelines within the scope of Organizational Culture, Advertising, Marketing, Sales and Distribution Channels, Product Designing, Policyholder Services, Underwriting, Confidentiality, Security of policyholder Information and Disclosure (in line with Personal Data Protection Act, No.9 of 2022 (PDPA)), Claims Handling, Complaint Handling and Dispute Resolution.
